Support

Need a hand with Kaia, found a bug, or have an idea? Email hello@trykaia.app and you'll hear back from a person, usually within a day or two.

Getting Kaia

Kaia is in beta on iPhone. Join through TestFlight, install it, and go for a drive.

Common questions

A drive didn't record

Check that Kaia has Location set to “Always” and Motion & Fitness turned on in iPhone Settings. You can also start a drive yourself with the record button.

A drive is on the wrong car

Open the drive, tap the menu, and choose Edit drive to change the car.

Deleting a drive

In Logs, select the drive and delete it.

Deleting your account

Go to your profile and choose Delete account. This removes your account and everything on our server. Your drives stay on your phone until you delete them or the app.

Reporting a bug

Email hello@trykaia.app with what happened and roughly when. A screenshot helps. On TestFlight you can also take a screenshot and tap Share Beta Feedback.
